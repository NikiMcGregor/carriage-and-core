ToeSox Half Toe Bellarina Grip Socks

Best for: Toe articulation in mat and reformer work
ToeSox · Grip socks · Price tier $$
  • Five-toe design with open (half) toes
  • Raised heel tab intended to protect the Achilles area
  • Non-slip sole the brand describes as patented
  • Organic cotton construction (retail listings cite 77% organic cotton)
  • Mary Jane–style strap with cutouts
Why consider it
  • Toes stay free to spread and grip
  • Heel tab helps the sock stay on during footwork
  • Widely sold by studio retailers
Trade-offs
  • Toe-sock fit is polarizing — some find the little-toe pocket awkward
  • Open toes leave toes uncovered on shared equipment
  • Single pairs are pricier than multipacks

Tavi Savvy Grip Socks

Best for: People who dislike toe socks and want a no-show look
Tavi (formerly Tavi Noir; sister brand of ToeSox) · Grip socks · Price tier $
  • Low-rise cut that sits below the ankle
  • Full-foot (closed-toe) design with a grip tread on the sole
  • Organic cotton knit (per retailer listings)
  • Marketed for barre, Pilates, yoga and dance
Why consider it
  • Conventional sock shape — no toe pockets
  • Discreet under leggings
  • Lower price point than most toe-sock styles
Trade-offs
  • Low cut can slip at the heel for some foot shapes
  • No arch band, so less hold than mid-calf styles during fast transitions

Bala Bangles (1 lb)

Best for: Adding light load to mat leg series and arm work
Bala · Ankle & wrist weights · Price tier $$
  • Sold as a pair, 1 lb each; a 2 lb version is also sold
  • Steel core wrapped in silicone
  • Hook-and-loop closure; worn on wrists or ankles
Why consider it
  • Easy on/off between exercises
  • Wipeable silicone surface
  • Fits wrists or ankles
Trade-offs
  • Only two weight options
  • More expensive than conventional sand-filled cuffs
