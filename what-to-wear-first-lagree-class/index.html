What to Wear to Your First Lagree Class

Your first Lagree class is not the time to discover that your top rides up in a plank or your socks slide in a lunge. The good news: the right outfit is simple, and you probably own most of it.

The checklist

  1. Fitted bottoms. Leggings or fitted shorts that won't catch on springs or the carriage's edge. Full-length or 7/8 both work; avoid wide legs.
  2. A top that stays put. You'll spend time in planks and with your head lower than your hips. A fitted tank or tee, or a top you can tuck into a high waistband, prevents flashing your midriff to the room.
  3. A supportive sports bra. Lagree is low-impact, so you don't need running-level support, but choose something comfortable through long holds.
  4. Grip socks. Most studios ask for them. If you only buy one item, make it this — and prefer a pair with a heel tab or arch band (see grip socks for Lagree).
  5. Hair tie and minimal jewellery. Rings and bracelets can scratch upholstery or pinch under handles.
  6. Water and a small towel. Classes are short but continuous.

On leggings

Choose fabric you won't think about. Very soft, low-compression leggings feel great for stretching but can shift during sustained holds; firmer, more compressive fabrics stay in place. Our leggings guide explains the trade-off.

What to leave at home

  • Loose, baggy layers (they catch and hide your alignment from the instructor)
  • Heavy perfume — studios are small
  • Anything you'd be upset to get sweaty

Before you go

Arrive early, tell the instructor it's your first class, and mention any injuries. Learn how to adjust the springs before class starts. Curious how Lagree differs from reformer Pilates? Read Pilates vs. Lagree.

Questions readers ask

Should I wear shoes to Lagree?

Classes are done in socks or bare feet on the machine. Wear any shoes you like to the studio and expect to take them off.

Can I wear shorts?

You can, but many people prefer leggings because lunges and kneeling work put skin in contact with shared upholstery and straps.
