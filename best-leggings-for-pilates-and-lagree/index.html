Best Leggings for Pilates and Lagree

Leggings for Pilates and Lagree have one job: disappear. If you're tugging at a waistband between exercises, they're the wrong pair. The right choice depends mostly on one variable — compression.

The compression trade-off

  • Low compression fabrics feel soft and barely-there. They're comfortable for stretching and slower mat work, but can shift during long, loaded holds.
  • Higher compression fabrics feel firmer and stay put during sustained work like Lagree lunges and planks, at the cost of a less relaxed feel.

Neither is "better". Many people own one of each.

Length and fit details

  • 25"–7/8 length keeps fabric clear of your ankles on the footbar.
  • High rise stays covered when you round forward.
  • Flat waistband won't press into your stomach when lying prone.
  • Lined light colours prevent sheerness.

Questions readers ask

What length leggings are best for reformer Pilates?

Cropped or 7/8 lengths keep fabric away from the footbar and straps, and they don't bunch at the ankle when you flex your feet.

How do I know if leggings are see-through?

Check in a deep forward fold in good light before class. Some brands line light colours; that detail is usually on the product page.
